Remaining must-fix (small)

Reveal frames. The right-pick scene zooms in so far that the cap is cropped, and the burst stars land on the eyes and face. In strip-win frames 1-2 two faces ghost over each other. Remove the zoom, keep the burst around the edges, and finish the crossfade before the burst peaks.
Landscape crop. The wide scene cuts the bed and body. In Play the toy blocks sit on the bottom edge, so the “sharing” object is nearly lost. Set object-position per moment, or use contain with a blurred fill.
Low-contrast wrong cards. In the square crop, the “hiding under blanket” card is almost white with a tiny child. Add a per-card focal point or zoom on the child, plus a thin inner border or shadow.
Enter state. In strip-enter the tray is blank, then a single smaller card pops in, then the layout settles. “Please wait…” is text for non-readers, and the try-again label shows with no text for a few frames. Reserve both card slots at final size, fade them in together, and show an icon only.
Play question mismatch. “Your friend wants to play” shows a lone boy and no friend. Since the art can’t be regenerated, reword the line (for example “Time to play! What should we do?”) or crop to suit.
