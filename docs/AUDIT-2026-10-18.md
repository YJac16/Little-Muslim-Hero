## Bugs / UX issues found
1. Helping caption said "Someone needs help" while the voice says "Mommy needs help" → caption aligned to voice. (fixed)
2. Desktop and phone-landscape: scene squeezed to a thin strip or removed entirely. (fixed: two-column landscape layout)
3. Tall portrait phones/tablets: blank gap under the choices. (fixed)
4. Toggling sound mid-moment restarted the narration; paused clips left callers waiting up to 12 s. (fixed)
5. After a refresh mid-game the background nasheed never started (autoplay blocked, no retry). (fixed: starts on first touch)
6. Hard left-right shake on a wrong pick is not "gentle". (replaced with a soft sway)
7. Not fixed / for decision: the wrong-choice art for Morning ("hiding under the blanket") and Bedtime ("jumping on the bed") does not show a "mine" situation, so a spoken "Mine" fits Play best. Helping/Meal are borderline.

## Animation audit → changes
Before: float on the mascot, hard shake, fade sparkles. Now: slow storybook scene drift + light sweep, staggered card pop-in with idle bob, win pop with star/crescent burst, soft sway on wrong pick, mascot emotion pop, caption cross-fade, dot-fill progress, moment slide-in, twinkling start screen, bouncing title, falling stars and hopping mascot on the end screen. All CSS only; `prefers-reduced-motion` already disables motion globally.
