## Bugs / UX issues found
1. Helping caption said "Someone needs help" while the voice says "Mommy needs help" → caption aligned to voice. (fixed)
2. Desktop and phone-landscape: scene squeezed to a thin strip or removed entirely. (fixed: two-column landscape layout)
3. Tall portrait phones/tablets: blank gap under the choices. (fixed)
4. Toggling sound mid-moment restarted the narration; paused clips left callers waiting up to 12 s. (fixed)
5. After a refresh mid-game the background nasheed never started (autoplay blocked, no retry). (fixed: starts on first touch)
6. Hard left-right shake on a wrong pick is not "gentle". (replaced with a soft sway)
7. Not fixed / for decision: the wrong-choice art for Morning ("hiding under the blanket") and Bedtime ("jumping on the bed") does not show a "mine" situation, so a spoken "Mine" fits Play best. Helping/Meal are borderline.

## Addendum 2: wrong-answer audio and loudness (18 Oct 2026, evening)
Founder clarification: the "mine" is just a sound played when the child picks the WRONG picture, and it should be there for every moment.

**Audit of every wrong-answer path (on `main`, same code as the live site):** 5 of 5 wrong pictures called `Preview-Mine.mp3` in code, and a headless-browser check confirmed a play on every wrong tap. So nothing was silent in code, but (a) it was the spoken word "Mine" in all five moments, which only matches Play Time ("Keeping the toys"), so it can feel like it only exists for one; (b) it was far louder than the narration (about -20 LUFS vs -30), and (c) the same clip every time. There were no other wrong-answer paths (no timeout/miss path, no third choice).

**Now:** one cue set in `public/assets/audio/wrong/` (narrator "Mine" + three synthesized soft cues "oh-oh", "boop", "sigh", all under 1 s). Each moment has its own order (`wrongCues` in `lib/levels.ts`); Play Time starts with "Mine", every other moment starts with a soft cue. Repeated wrong picks rotate, never the same cue twice in a row. Cues are preloaded.

**Loudness:** all game clips loudness-matched (`tools-audio/normalize.py`): voices -20 LUFS, wrong cues -21, success chime/twinkle/whoosh/celebrate -26, blips peak -14 dBFS, every true peak at or below -2 dBFS. Before: narration -29 to -36, Mine -20.5, success chime -37, celebrate -50 LUFS; Gentle-pop-for-retry peaked at -0.7 dBFS (unused).

**Check:** `python3 tools-audio/verify_wrong_picks.py http://localhost:4173/` drives the static build in headless Chrome, makes 4 wrong picks in each of the 5 moments, and fails if any has no cue, or a cue file is missing or longer than 1 s.
